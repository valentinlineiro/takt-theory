## 1. Active Exploration Policy & Stopping Criteria

1. **EVSI Priority Threshold:** Candidates prioritized by boundary proximity $1 / (1 + d_{\text{boundary}})$ and spatial density bonus $1 / (1 + \text{neighbors})$.
2. **20% Uniform Random Exploration Quota:** 20% of sampled points strictly allocated to uniform grid sampling to prevent adaptive sampling bias.
3. **Stopping Criteria:** Active sampling halts when the classification uncertainty $U(\hat{f}_1) < 0.05$ across the 3D phase space $(k, \Delta D, n)$.

---

## 2. Theoretical vs. Estimated Boundary Distinction

- **$f_1(k, \Delta D, n) = 0$:** Theoretical geometry derived from TAKT v1.0.
- **$\hat{f}_1(k, \Delta D, n) = 0$:** Empirically estimated boundary surface generated by active sampling.
