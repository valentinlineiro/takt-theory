# Bibliography — TAKT Novelty Audit

## Sufficient Statistics

- Berger, J. O. (1985). *Statistical Decision Theory and Bayesian Analysis*.
  Springer.
- Lehmann, E. L. (1983). *Theory of Point Estimation*. Wiley.
- Neyman, J. & Pearson, E. S. (1933). On the problem of the most efficient
  tests of statistical hypotheses. *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal
  Society of London A*, 231, 289–337.
- Fisher, R. A. (1922). On the mathematical foundations of theoretical
  statistics. *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society of London A*,
  222, 309–368.
- Le Cam, L. (1964). Sufficiency and approximate sufficiency. *The Annals of
  Mathematical Statistics*, 35(4), 1419–1455.

## Blackwell

- Blackwell, D. (1951). Comparison of experiments. *Proceedings of the Second
  Berkeley Symposium on Mathematical Statistics and Probability*, 93–102.
- Blackwell, D. (1953). Equivalent comparisons of experiments. *The Annals of
  Mathematical Statistics*, 24(2), 265–272.
- Le Cam, L. (1964). Sufficiency and approximate sufficiency. *The Annals of
  Mathematical Statistics*, 35(4), 1419–1455.

## Information Bottleneck

- Tishby, N., Pereira, F. C., & Bialek, W. (1999). The information bottleneck
  method. *Proceedings of the 37th Annual Allerton Conference on Communication,
  Control, and Computing*, 368–377.
- Tishby, N. & Polani, D. (2011). Information theory of decisions and actions.
  In *Perception-Action Cycle: Models, Architectures, and Hardware*. Springer,
  601–636.
- Tishby, N. & Zaslavsky, N. (2015). Deep learning and the information
  bottleneck principle. *IEEE Information Theory Workshop (ITW)*, 1–5.
- Still, S. & Precup, D. (2012). An information-theoretic approach to
  curiosity-driven reinforcement learning. *Theory in Biosciences*, 131(3),
  139–148.
- Fox, R., Pakman, A., & Tishby, N. (2016). Taming the noise in reinforcement
  learning via soft updates. *UAI*.
- Grau-Moya, J., Leibfried, F., & Vrancx, P. (2018). Soft Q-learning with
  mutual information regularization. *ICLR*.
- Dubois, Y., Kiela, D., Schwab, D. J., & Vedantam, R. (2020). Learning
  optimal representations with the decodable information bottleneck.
  *NeurIPS*.
- Kamatsuka, A. & Yoshida, T. (2026). A generalized information bottleneck
  method: A decision-theoretic perspective. *arXiv:2602.18405*.

- Larsen, K. G. & Skou, A. (1991). Bisimulation through probabilistic testing.

- Park, D. (1981). Concurrency and automata on infinite sequences.
  *Theoretical Computer Science*, 104–116.
- Milner, R. (1989). *Communication and Concurrency*. Prentice Hall.
