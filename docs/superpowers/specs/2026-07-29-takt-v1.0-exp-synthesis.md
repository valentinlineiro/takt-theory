## 3. Scope of Empirical Evidence vs. Unresolved Boundaries

### 3.1 Verified Empirical Properties (Demonstrated Support)
1. **Decision Margin Preservation:** Verified that conservative policies maintain significantly higher decision margins ($0.5400$ vs $0.0840$) and prevent degradation transitions (`BENCHMARK-001`).
2. **Dimensional Projection Invariance:** Demonstrated that computing decision margins and evaluating governance steps is invariant under state vector scaling ($|S| \in \{2, 10, 50, 100, 500, 1000\}$) when projected through a task capability kernel (`BENCHMARK-002`).
3. **Deterministic Horizon Boundary ($H_{\text{bound}}$):** Demonstrated that trajectories under steady environmental drift ($\theta = 0.05$) transition deterministically to `DEGRADED` states at exactly $H_{\text{bound}} = 10$ steps without incurring contract violations (`VIOLATION = 0`) (`BENCHMARK-003`).

### 3.2 Unresolved Boundaries & Open Questions
1. **Multi-Agent / Asynchronous Node Delay:** Behavior under non-zero communication delay between distributed nodes remains unexercised empirically.
2. **Sub-Capability Granularity ($Sound(T, c, R)$):** Relation granularity finer than discrete capability sets remains unformalized (ST-017 Q2).
3. **Multi-Contract Composition:** Simultaneous evaluation of competing, multi-objective contract horizons under dynamic drift.

## 4. Triggers for Next Theoretical Frontier (ST-018)

New theoretical modules (such as ST-018) **MUST NOT** be created by feature expansion. ST-018 will be initiated **ONLY** upon encountering one of the following empirical triggers:
1. **Adversarial Communication Delay:** Empirical refutation of governance horizon guarantees when node state synchronization experiences stochastic delay ($\tau_{\text{delay}} > 0$).
2. **Multi-Contract Deadlock:** Observation of unresolvable state machine deadlocks during simultaneous multi-contract recalibration.
